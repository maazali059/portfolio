Group Captive Structure

26% equity / 51% self-consumption are the thresholds most commonly cited under the captive generation framework (Electricity Rules, as amended) — confirm against the current rule text and any state-commission interpretation before relying on them; this model does not assert they are current law.
Generation is computed hourly from the site's own solar CUF-shaped × solar MW + wind CUF-shaped × wind MW (per the split above). Equity ownership share, energy entitlement, and actual self-consumption are tracked as three separate numbers — owning 26% of the SPV does not automatically mean receiving 26% of its energy; the entitlement field above is an explicit, editable assumption, not derived from equity %.

FPPPA/FPPAS is a genuinely separate, periodically-revised (typically quarterly) surcharge that GERC requires DISCOMs to show as its own line item on the bill — it is NOT already included in the "Energy charge" figure above and must NOT be left at 0 for a real decision. Grid tariffs are DISCOM- and category-specific (commercial / EV public charging category where notified) and change with each tariff order — treat every value here as a placeholder pending the current schedule. 8,760-Hour Dispatch Engine Canonical

This is the ONE dispatch calculation used everywhere else in the model (Economics, BESS, Grid, Decision, Break-Even, Optimizer). BESS Value Stack & Sizing Optimiser

BESS MW and MWh are independent decision variables (linked only by the min/max C-rate feasibility band below, not fixed to a single C-rate). The model sweeps candidate MW×MWh combinations and finds where marginal value stops exceeding marginal cost, using the full 8,760h engine.

BESS Parameters

MW and MWh are independent decision variables in the optimiser/sweep (searched on a grid, e.g. 5MW/10MWh vs 5MW/20MWh vs 10MW/20MWh) — implied C-rate (MW/MWh) is checked against the min/max range above and infeasible combinations are rejected before scoring, rather than MW being derived from MWh × a fixed C-rate.
